# Natural material sources

Shared 1K JPG albedo maps, downloaded from Poly Haven. All four are CC0:

| Local file | Source |
| --- | --- |
| `natural-bark-albedo.jpg` | [Brown Bark 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_01) |
| `natural-ground-albedo.jpg` | [Forest Leaves 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_leaves_02) |
| `natural-rock-albedo.jpg` | [Mossy Rock](https://polyhaven.com/a/mossy_rock) |
| `natural-concrete-albedo.jpg` | [Concrete Wall 001](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_wall_001) |

`natural-foliage-atlas.png` is a generated transparent leaf atlas; its generation
brief is preserved in `natural-foliage-atlas.txt`. Frog skin, feather detail,
lily veins and grass veins are generated by the runtime. No animal model downloads
or additional per-animal lights are required.

Texture objects are shared by surface family. Ground detail blends three rotated
scales with low contrast, only on land. Fable's underwater bowl is not textured.
